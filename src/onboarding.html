Get started by pinning the extension to your toolbar

How to Pin the Extension

1
Click the puzzle piece icon

Look for the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in your Chrome toolbar, usually in the top-right corner next to your profile picture.

2
Find "Click to Markdown"

In the extensions menu that appears, locate "Click to Markdown" in the list of installed extensions.

3
Click the pin icon

Click the pin icon (📌) next to "Click to Markdown" to pin it to your toolbar. The extension icon will now appear directly in your toolbar for quick access!

💡 Why pin it? Pinning the extension makes it much easier to access. Instead of opening the extensions menu every time, you can click the icon directly from your toolbar.
